Frequently Asked Questions

1. What types of files does Quick Access for Docs support?

Quick Access for Docs is specifically designed for Google Workspace documents including Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Slides, Google Forms, and Google Drawings. You can also browse and manage files in your My Drive folder.

2. Is my data safe?

Absolutely. Quick Access for Docs uses Google's official OAuth 2.0 authentication and communicates directly with Google Drive APIs. We never store your credentials or access your data on our servers.

3. Can I edit documents within the app?

Yes! Quick Access for Docs includes a built-in document viewer that allows you to open and edit Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides directly within the application using Google's web editor.

4. How many accounts can I add?

You can add and manage multiple Google accounts, switching between them seamlessly within the app.

5. What formats can I export documents to?

Google Docs can be exported to DOCX or PDF. Google Sheets can be exported to XLSX or CSV. Google Slides can be exported to PPTX or PDF. Google Forms export to PDF, and Google Drawings export to PNG.

6. Can I use Quick Access for Docs offline?

Quick Access for Docs requires an internet connection to access your Google Drive documents. However, downloaded files are available offline on your Mac.

7. What is the Ownership Filter?

The Ownership Filter allows you to quickly filter documents by ownership - view only files you own, only files shared with you by others, or all files regardless of ownership.
