How It Works

1. Connect Your Account

Sign in with your Google account using secure OAuth 2.0 authentication. Quick Access never stores your password.

2. Browse Your Files

Navigate through your Google Drive folders with an intuitive interface. Switch between list and grid views based on your preference.

3. Manage Your Files

Download, upload, organize, and share your files with powerful batch operations and queue management.

4. Track Your Progress

Monitor all download and upload operations in real-time with detailed progress indicators and speed metrics.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Quick Access free?

Quick Access offers both free and premium features. Basic file management is available to all users, with advanced features available through in-app purchase.

2. Is my data safe?

Absolutely. Quick Access uses Google's official OAuth 2.0 authentication and communicates directly with Google Drive APIs. We never store your credentials or access your data on our servers.

3. Can I use Quick Access offline?

Quick Access requires an internet connection to access your Google Drive files. However, downloaded files are available offline on your Mac.

4. How many accounts can I add?

You can add and manage multiple Google accounts, switching between them seamlessly within the app.

5. What file types are supported?

Quick Access supports all file types stored in Google Drive, including Google Workspace documents (Docs, Sheets, Slides), images, videos, PDFs, and more.

6. Can I upload folders?

Yes! Quick Access supports uploading entire folders while preserving their structure, including nested subfolders.

7. What happens if a download or upload fails?

Failed tasks can be easily retried with one click. Quick Access also provides detailed error messages to help you understand what went wrong.
