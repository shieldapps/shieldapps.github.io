Frequently Asked Questions

1. What Gmail features does Quick Access for Gmail support?

Quick Access for Gmail supports browsing emails, searching, organizing with labels, batch operations (archive, delete, mark read/unread), category tabs, and multi-account management. For composing and replying to emails, Quick Access for Gmail opens Gmail in your browser for the full editing experience.

2. Is my email data safe?

Absolutely. Quick Access for Gmail uses Google's official OAuth 2.0 authentication and communicates directly with Gmail APIs. We never store your emails or credentials on our servers. All data remains in your Gmail account.

3. Can I compose emails within the app?

Quick Access for Gmail focuses on efficient email browsing and management. For composing, replying, and forwarding emails, the app opens Gmail in your default browser, giving you access to Gmail's full editing capabilities including rich text formatting, attachments, and signatures.

4. How many accounts can I add?

You can add and manage multiple Gmail accounts, switching between them seamlessly within the app. Each account maintains its own independent settings and authentication.

5. What are Category Tabs?

Category Tabs are Gmail's smart classification system that automatically sorts your emails into Primary (personal), Social (social networks), Promotions (marketing), Updates (receipts and bills), and Forums (mailing lists). Quick Access for Gmail displays these tabs when viewing your Inbox.

6. Can I use Quick Access for Gmail offline?

Quick Access for Gmail requires an internet connection to access your Gmail. Since emails are stored in your Gmail account (not locally), an active connection is needed to browse and manage your messages.

7. Does Quick Access for Gmail support Gmail filters and rules?

Quick Access for Gmail respects all Gmail filters and rules you've set up in Gmail. Any automatic labeling, forwarding, or filtering configured in Gmail will work seamlessly with Quick Access for Gmail.
